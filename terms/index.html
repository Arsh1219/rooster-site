Alarms: please read this one

Rooster is built to be reliable, but every alarm ultimately depends on your device. The app must remain installed, alarm permission must stay granted, and your iPhone or iPad must be powered on and functioning. Whether an alarm is heard also depends on your device's settings and condition, including volume and sound settings, Attention Aware features, connected Bluetooth devices or headphones, battery level, Low Power Mode, iOS updates and restarts.

iOS stops any alarm that is left ringing for about 15 minutes. Rooster adds one backup ring about 16 minutes after the alarm, but it is a single extra ring, not a guarantee. The wake check and the bedtime battery warning need notification permission and depend on iOS delivering them on time. Use the alarm test in Settings to check that your setup is loud enough.

Do not rely on any alarm app as your only safeguard for critical events such as flights, medication, exams or work shifts. To the maximum extent permitted by law, we are not liable for losses caused by an alarm that did not sound, sounded late, or was dismissed.

Lock before ring

When you turn on lock before ring, you cannot turn off, delete, skip, pause or change the time of that alarm during the lock window you chose. The lock opens when the alarm rings. Only turn it on if you are sure you want that alarm to ring.

Missions, movement and safety

Some missions ask you to move: walk, shake your phone, do pushups or squats, or find something in your home. You choose whether to use them and how many reps or steps to set. Only do what is safe for you, make sure the space around you is clear and well lit, and do not use exercise missions if you have a health condition or injury that makes them unsafe. Rooster is not a fitness or medical product.

Photo match, QR scan, Item hunt, Pushups and Squats use your camera, and Steps and Shake use motion sensors. Recognition runs on your device and may sometimes fail to recognise an object, a movement or a photo. NFC tags and printed codes you use with a mission are your own responsibility.

Every mission has an escape: after a few missed tries, or at once if the camera or a sensor is unavailable, hold for ten seconds and type the phrase shown, and the alarm stops. A mission may make dismissal effortful, never impossible.
