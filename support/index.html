Common questions

My alarm did not ring

Rooster rings through Silent, Do Not Disturb and every Focus using Apple's alarm system, but it needs the alarm permission. If the app says alarms cannot ring, tap Fix it and turn alarms back on, or open iOS Settings, Rooster and allow alarms there. Keep the app installed, because deleting it deletes its alarms. A device that is switched off or out of battery cannot ring.

It rang too quietly

In the app, open Settings, Test your alarm. It rings a real alarm in ten seconds so you can hear how loud your setup is. If it is quiet, raise Ring and Alerts in iOS Settings under Sounds and Haptics. On iPhones with Face ID, turning off Attention Aware Features stops a glance at the screen from lowering the sound.

Does it ring if I swipe the app away?

Yes. Rooster still rings after a force quit or a restart, with a basic alarm sound until you open the app again. Leave Rooster in the background to wake to your chosen sound.

Do I need notifications?

Not for the alarm itself. Allow them for the safety nets: the wake check that asks if you are still up, and the warning before you sleep when the battery looks too low to last the night or the alarm permission has been turned off.

A mission cannot be finished

Every mission has an escape hatch. After three missed tries or four minutes, or at once if the camera or a sensor is unavailable, hold for ten seconds and type "I give up". The alarm stops.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open your App Store subscriptions to change or cancel. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

I bought Pro but it is locked

In the app, go to Settings, Restore Purchases, or tap Restore on the Pro screen. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What data do you collect?

Very little. There is no account, no ads and no tracking across apps. The app sends usage signals and crash reports so we can fix bugs, plus install and purchase signals so we know whether our ads work, all under random identifiers that do not name you. Details are in the privacy policy.
