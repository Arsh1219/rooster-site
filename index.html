FAQ

Fair questions

Will it really ring on Silent?

Yes. Rooster schedules alarms through AlarmKit, Apple's system alarm framework, the same machinery behind the Clock app. The Silent switch, Do Not Disturb and Focus modes do not apply to system alarms.

What if a mission is impossible in the moment?

Hold 10 seconds and type "I give up". The escape hatch is on every mission screen and always works, even with a broken camera or a missing NFC tag.

Does it work on Android or iPad?

No. Rooster is iPhone only and needs iOS 26 or later, because AlarmKit exists only there.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free forever: alarms that ring through Silent, repeat schedules, labels, and snooze rules. Pro adds the missions, stacking, escalation, wake check, shift rotations and imported sounds. $24.99 a year or $34.99 once.

What do you collect about me?

There is no account and there are no ads. Mission photos never leave your phone. Rooster sends anonymous crash and usage signals, nothing that identifies you. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open your App Store subscriptions and cancel there. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.
