FAQ

Fair questions

Will it really ring on Silent?

Yes. Rooster schedules alarms through AlarmKit, Apple's system alarm framework, the same machinery behind the Clock app. The Silent switch, Do Not Disturb and Focus modes do not apply to system alarms.

What if a mission is impossible in the moment?

After three missed tries or four minutes, hold ten seconds and type "I give up". If the camera, a sensor or the NFC tag fails, the escape hatch is there at once, and it always works.

Does it work on iPad or Android?

Rooster runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, because AlarmKit exists only there. There is no Android version.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free forever: alarms that ring through Silent, repeat schedules, labels, snooze rules, a nap timer, a test ring, skip dates and a vacation pause. Pro adds all eleven missions and stacking, escalation, the wake check, shift rotations, lock before ring, the sound pack and imported sounds. Plans and prices are shown in the app.

What do you collect about me?

There is no account and there are no ads. Mission photos and camera frames never leave your phone. Rooster sends crash reports, usage signals and the install and purchase signals that tell us whether our ads work, under random identifiers, nothing that names you. The privacy policy has the details.

Which languages does it speak?

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese and Traditional Chinese. Pick one in Settings, or follow your device.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open your App Store subscriptions and cancel there. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.
